Database Schema Notes for Analysts

Tables:
1) customers — who buys from us (name, email, city, signup_date, status)
2) employees — internal staff (department, role, salary, manager hierarchy)
3) projects — delivery work linked to a customer and a lead employee
4) sales — revenue events linked to customer, employee, optional project

Joins:
- sales.customer_id -> customers.customer_id
- sales.employee_id -> employees.employee_id
- projects.customer_id -> customers.customer_id
- projects.lead_employee_id -> employees.employee_id

If SQL cannot answer a conceptual/policy question, use these notes.
