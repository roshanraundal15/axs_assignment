AXS Internal Knowledge Base — Company Overview

AXS is a technology company focused on AI/ML solutions for enterprise clients.
Headquarters: Mumbai, India.
Founded: 2018.
Core products: Analytics Suite, Cloud License, Support Plan, API Credits, Training packages.

Sales regions: West, South, North, East, Central.
Fiscal year runs from April to March.
Customer status values: active, inactive.
Project status values: active, completed, on_hold.

Business rules:
- Top customers are ranked by total sales amount.
- Engineering department typically has the highest average salaries.
- Q1 means January–March, Q2 April–June, Q3 July–September, Q4 October–December (calendar year for this demo database).
- "Last year" means the previous calendar year relative to today.
