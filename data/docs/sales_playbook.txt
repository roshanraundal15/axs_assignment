AXS Sales Playbook (Internal Notes)

When analyzing sales:
- Always prefer aggregations by region or product for executive summaries.
- A large deal is typically above 200,000 INR in this demo dataset scale.
- Support Plan and Cloud License are the most common products.
- Sales can optionally link to a project via project_id.

FAQ:
Q: How do we define top customers?
A: Sum of sales.amount grouped by customer, ordered descending.

Q: What is an active project?
A: projects.status = 'active'.

Q: How to compute average salary?
A: AVG(salary) from employees, optionally GROUP BY department.
